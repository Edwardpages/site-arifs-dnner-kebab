About Arif's Dönner Kebab

Founded in 2026 by Arif, our restaurant brings authentic Turkish flavours to Berlin. Every dish is crafted with passion, using the freshest ingredients and traditional recipes.

Our mission is simple: serve unforgettable dönner kebab that keeps you coming back for more.

About Arif's Dönner Kebab

Founded in 2026, Arif's Dönner Kebab brings authentic Turkish flavours to Berlin. Our secret lies in the perfect blend of spices, freshly sliced meat and a dash of love. Whether you're after a quick bite or a relaxed dinner, our doors are always open for kebab lovers.

We pride ourselves on quality, speed and a friendly atmosphere. Come experience the taste that keeps our customers coming back for more.

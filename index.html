About Arif's Dönner Kebab

Founded in 2026 by Arif, our restaurant brings authentic Turkish street food to the heart of Berlin. Every kebab is crafted with love, using premium cuts and traditional spices. Whether you’re after a quick bite or a relaxed dinner, we welcome you to experience the vibrant flavours of the Mediterranean.

Our commitment to quality extends to every dessert, especially our handmade baklava, and our friendly staff who are always ready to serve with a smile.
